# Privacy Policy

*Last updated: 11 November 2026*

Pagewrit is a library app for the books and documents already on your Mac. This page explains what it does with your information. The short version: your library stays on your Mac, and Pagewrit collects nothing about you.

## What Pagewrit stores, and where

Your library, bookmarks, highlights, notes, tags, ratings, reading progress, reading goal and streaks are stored only on your Mac. Pagewrit reads files only in the folders you choose to let it scan. It never uploads your files, your library or your reading history.

Backups you make are files saved where you choose, on your Mac or in a folder you pick. Pagewrit does not send them anywhere.

## What Pagewrit does not do

There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Pagewrit does not sell or share personal data, because it does not collect any.

## iCloud Drive and Google Drive

If you add an iCloud Drive or Google Drive folder, Pagewrit reads it the same way as any other folder, through the copy your Mac keeps. Downloading and syncing is done by Apple or Google, under their own privacy policies. Pagewrit does not sign in to either service and never sees your account details.

## When Pagewrit goes online

Only when you ask it to:

- **Fix Metadata** sends the title you type to Open Library to find matching book details and covers.
- **Download Books** sends your search to Project Gutenberg, the Internet Archive and Open Library, and downloads the book you choose.

Those services can see your IP address, as any website would, and their own privacy policies apply to them.

## Crash reports

If Pagewrit crashes, macOS may produce a technical crash report. Pagewrit can offer to include it in an email to support. You choose whether to send it. Reports contain technical details about the app only, never your library, file names or file paths.

## Support emails

If you email support, the message can include the app version and macOS version. It never includes your library, file names or file paths.

## Wellbeing features

Break reminders, quiet hours, reading goals and streak badges run entirely on your Mac. They are stored with your settings and are never sent anywhere.

## Children

Pagewrit does not collect information from anyone, including children.

## Removing your data

Delete Pagewrit and its data folder to remove everything it has stored. Your original books are never moved or changed unless you choose Delete File.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it.
