# Privacy

*Last updated: 20 September 2026*

Pagewrit is a library app for the books already on your Mac.

**What stays on your Mac.** Your books, your library, bookmarks, tags, shelves and reading progress are stored only on your Mac. Pagewrit reads files only in the folders you let it scan. It never uploads your files, library or reading history.

**What Pagewrit does not do.** There are no accounts, no analytics, no tracking and no ads.

**When Pagewrit goes online.** Only when you ask it to:

- *Fix Metadata* sends the title you type to Open Library to find matching book details and covers.
- *Download Books* sends your search to Project Gutenberg, the Internet Archive and Open Library, and downloads the book you choose.

Those services can see your IP address, as any website would, and their own privacy policies apply to them.

**Support.** If you email support, the message includes the app version and macOS version. It never includes your library, file names or file paths.

**Removing your data.** Delete Pagewrit and its data folder to remove everything it has stored. Your original books are never moved or changed unless you choose Delete File.

Questions: see the support contact in Settings, under About & privacy.
